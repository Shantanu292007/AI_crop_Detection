Frequently Asked Questions

Is this system sending images to an external server? No. In this version, all diagnosis processing and image rendering run strictly inside your browser. No files leave your computer.
Can I test this without having crop photos? Yes! The Diagnosis page includes 4 one-click sample leaves (Tomato Blight, Healthy Corn, Potato Blight, Apple Spot).
