✦ For better results:

  • ✓
    Use natural daylight:

    Overcast or early morning sunlight provides the clearest contrast without color distortion.

  • ✓
    Keep the leaf in sharp focus:

    Hold your camera steady around 15–25 cm away from the leaf blade.

  • ✓
    Avoid blurry images:

    Motion blur prevents algorithmic edge and lesion pattern extraction.

  • ✓
    Include the affected area clearly:

    Capture the boundary between diseased tissue and healthy leaf green.

  • ✓
    Use a single leaf when possible:

    Single leaf close-ups yield substantially higher diagnostic accuracy than whole-canopy shots.
